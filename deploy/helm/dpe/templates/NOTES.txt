Run a job:

  kubectl apply -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} -f examples/processingjob.yaml
  kubectl get processingjobs -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} -w

The REPLICAS, READY, LAG and PENDING columns come from the job's status
subresource, which the operator refreshes every 5 seconds from the
coordinator's GetClusterStats.

Submit work through the control plane (requires grpcurl):

  kubectl port-forward -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} svc/{{ include "dpe.coordinatorName" . }} {{ .Values.coordinator.grpcPort }}:{{ .Values.coordinator.grpcPort }} &
  grpcurl -plaintext localhost:{{ .Values.coordinator.grpcPort }} engine.v1.Engine/GetClusterStats

Workers scale on consumer-group lag: desired replicas is
ceil(streamLag / autoscale.targetLagPerWorker), clamped to
[minReplicas, maxReplicas]. Scaling out is immediate; scaling in waits for the
stabilization window and then drains the highest-ordinal worker before its pod
is removed.
